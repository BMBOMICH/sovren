# Sovren Privacy Policy

**Effective date: 5 September 2026**

Sovren is a local programming-language compiler. The compiler does not include
telemetry, advertising, analytics, account login, or a service that transmits
source code or personal data to the developer.

## Data handled by the compiler

Sovren reads source files and libraries selected by the user and writes build
outputs to locations selected by the user. This processing occurs on the user's
device. The compiler does not upload those files.

Sovren may receive ordinary command-line arguments and operating-system error
information needed to perform a requested build. These values are processed
locally and are not sent to the developer by the compiler.

## Programs created with Sovren

Programs compiled with Sovren are separate works controlled by their authors.
Such a program may collect or transmit data if its author deliberately writes it
to do so. That program's author is responsible for its behavior and privacy
notice; this policy does not cover third-party programs merely because Sovren
compiled them.

## Microsoft Store

Microsoft may process Store account, purchase, installation, update, diagnostic,
or reliability data under Microsoft's own terms and privacy statement. The
Sovren compiler does not receive Microsoft account credentials or Store payment
information.

## Retention and sharing

Because the compiler does not transmit personal data to the developer, the
developer does not retain or sell compiler telemetry or user source code. Files
created locally remain subject to the user's own storage, backup, and deletion
choices.

## Children

Sovren is a general development tool and does not knowingly collect personal
information from children.

## Changes

Material changes will be published with a new effective date. A release uses the
policy available through its official Store listing or official repository.

## Contact

Privacy questions should be sent through the support contact shown on Sovren's
official Microsoft Store listing. Do not include passwords, private signing
keys, identity documents, or source code unrelated to the question.
